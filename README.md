## How I work

Curiosity is useful only when it is paired with boundaries. My approach is simple: stay within scope, protect people and data, document what happened, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and escalate when the risk calls for it.

I have learned that responsible security work is less about looking fearless and more about being dependable — especially when a situation is unclear. That is the kind of analyst and teammate I am working to become.
